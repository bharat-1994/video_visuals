# Card A: step-through scooter (easy)
File: `asset_scooter.py`, function `draw(ctx, x, y, s=1.0)`. Side view, facing RIGHT. (x, y) = bottom-centre (the ground contact line between the wheels).
Size at s=1: width 300-380 px, height 190-250 px. Everything scales with s.
Real thing: a classic Italian-style step-through scooter (think 1960s), generic, no brand.
Signature features (all must be recognisable):
1. Two small wheels with tyre, rim and hub (front wheel under a fender)
2. Rounded front leg-shield (apron) rising from the floor up to the handlebar, curved like an S
3. Low flat floorboard between the shield and the rear body (the "step-through")
4. Round headlight on the handlebar top, plus a small mirror stalk
5. Bulging rear engine cowl, rounded, above the rear wheel
6. Long single saddle sitting on top of the cowl
7. Tail light (small red) at the back and a short exhaust/muffler under the cowl
8. Two-tone body (one main colour + shadow side) with a lighter highlight stripe
Allowed text: none. Banned: any brand name or logo.
