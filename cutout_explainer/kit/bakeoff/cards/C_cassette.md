4. A row of 4-5 chunky transport buttons below the window; the PLAY button is larger/triangle-marked
5. A volume wheel (ridged circle) on the top-right edge and a small headphone jack
6. Headphones: a curved band plus two round foam pads (orange or red), lying beside/above the player
7. A thin cable running from the jack to the headphones
8. A small label strip with plain handwritten-style lines (no words) under the window
